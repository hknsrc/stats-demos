# Interactive Research Methods Studio

This repository contains a set of small interactive visualizations designed to help students develop intuition about key concepts in quantitative methods and factor analysis.

The demos aim to make abstract statistical ideas more visually intuitive and easier to understand in teaching contexts.

## Interactive demos

The demos are grouped into four topics:

**Statistical Inference**
- What 95% Confidence Means
- What Determines Power?
- Optional Stopping
- Bonferroni & False Discovery Rate

**Regression & Research Designs**
- Group × Time Interaction
- Regression to the Mean
- Simpson’s Paradox
- Simple Slopes & Johnson–Neyman
- Direct & Indirect Effects (Mediation)

**Measurement & Reliability**
- Measurement Error & Attenuation
- Cronbach’s Alpha & Omega
- Item Response Curves

**Factor Analysis & SEM**
- EFA Motion Demo
- How Many Factors? (Parallel Analysis)
- Rotation Intuition
- ESEM Visualization / Mini Game
- Measurement Invariance

## Interactive Exercise & Health Behavior Studio

A second collection, in `health-behavior/`, covers theories of behavior change for undergraduate courses in exercise psychology and health behavior. Every demo follows the same fictional student, Jordan, who is trying to walk 150 minutes a week.

**Motivation & Intention**
- Why Intention Isn’t Action (Theory of Planned Behavior)
- Why Does Jordan Walk? (Self-Determination Theory)

**Habits & Self-Regulation**
- How Long Does a Habit Take? (Habit Formation)

Live: https://hknsrc.github.io/stats-demos/health-behavior/
