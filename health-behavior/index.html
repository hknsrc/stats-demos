The student in every demo

Meet Jordan

Each demo looks at the same situation through one theory, so the theories can be compared rather than memorized one by one.

Who
A second-year university student, mostly inactive since exam season.
Why now
At a routine check-up, Jordan’s blood pressure was borderline and the doctor recommended regular activity.
Goal
150 minutes of brisk walking a week.
Barriers
A busy timetable, the weather, friends who don’t exercise, and often feeling too tired.
